# Demo script for Haqdar

Target length: 4 to 5 minutes. Record on a phone or in a narrow browser window, with the sound on. Speak Hindi to Saathi so the judges hear a real Indian language. Keep the app in Hindi.

## Before you record

1. Log out and log in as the demo account: number 9999999999, password saathi123. In the menu, tap "Replay the intro" so the intro shows again.
2. Set your phone to forward missed and busy calls to Saathi: dial `**61*+918031450668#` and `**67*+918031450668#`.
3. Keep a second phone ready to call your number during the recording.
4. Have a payslip screenshot ready on the phone (any delivery app earnings screen).
5. Open WhatsApp with the chat to +1 555 842 9358 ready, from the number you signed up with.

## Scene 1: The problem (20 seconds)

Say: "Delivery riders get customer calls while they ride. Answering is dangerous. Missing the call brings a penalty. They also do not know their pay, their rights, or how to complain. Haqdar fixes this with Saathi, a voice co-pilot that works in nine Indian languages."

## Scene 2: Meet Saathi (45 seconds)

1. Show the login screen, log in, and skip to the intro.
2. Tap "Meet Saathi". Allow the microphone. Saathi greets you by name in Hindi and explains what it does. Let the judges see the mouth move with the voice.
3. Say in Hindi: "Haan, test call karte hain."

## Scene 3: Saathi answers a real call (60 seconds)

1. Tap "Start riding". Say: "While I ride, calls go to Saathi."
2. From the second phone, call your number. Do not answer. The call forwards to Saathi. Speak like a customer: give a gate code and ask when the order arrives. Saathi answers with your live ETA and takes the message.
3. Hang up. Back on the app, tap "I have stopped". Saathi reads the message aloud: who called, the gate code, what they want. Show the call card with the masked number. If a recording is attached, tap play.

## Scene 4: Hands-free actions (40 seconds)

Tap "Talk to Saathi" and say in Hindi, in one breath: "Aaj 850 rupaye kamaye 9 ghante mein, petrol 150. Ab Koramangala ja raha hoon, 15 minute lagenge." Saathi logs the earnings, sets the order and ETA, and confirms. Open the Money tab to show the day logged and the fair pay check with the minimum wage.

## Scene 5: Help in simple words (50 seconds)

1. Open Help, then "Explain a photo or message". Upload the payslip screenshot. Saathi explains the deductions in Hindi and offers next steps. Tap "Add to my earnings".
2. Go back, open "My benefits and rights". Enter age and income. Show the schemes with apply steps and official links.
3. Go back, open "Write a complaint". Type one line about a wrongly blocked account. Show the formal letter, the summary in Hindi, and the WhatsApp share button.

## Scene 6: Saathi on WhatsApp (40 seconds)

1. Switch to WhatsApp. Send a text in Hindi: "Aaj 900 kamaye 8 ghante mein, petrol 120. Ab Indiranagar ja raha hoon, 12 minute. Aur e-Shram card kya hai?"
2. Show the reply: Saathi logs the day, sets the order, answers the question, and sends a voice note. Play the voice note.
3. If voice notes and photos are confirmed working before you record, send a voice note and a payslip photo too. Otherwise skip them.

## Scene 7: Close (20 seconds)

Say: "Every answer is a real Gemini call: Gemini Live for the voice, Gemini Flash for photos, text, and tools, Gemini text to speech for the replies. Keys stay on the server. Data is per user. 62 tests run in CI. Haqdar gives information, not legal advice."

## If something fails on camera

- If the mic is blocked, type to Saathi in the box under the buttons. It answers the same way.
- If the call does not forward, tap "Play my messages" to replay the last real call.
- If Saathi is slow, keep talking to the camera. The reply usually lands within ten seconds.
